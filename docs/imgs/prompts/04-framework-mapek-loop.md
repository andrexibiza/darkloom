MAPE-K Autonomous Self-Healing Loop — Darkloom Governance Architecture

STRUCTURE: Five-node circular diagram with bidirectional Knowledge connections

NODES (clockwise, starting at top):

- ANALYZE (Top-right): "Calculate drift scores — expected vs observed behavior". Icon: delta/difference symbol
- PLAN (Bottom-right): "Determine remediation — auto-fix, draft PR, or escalate to human". Icon: decision tree
- EXECUTE (Bottom-left): "Apply fix or revoke autonomy based on trust threshold". Icon: gear/execution

  "Junior: Low Risk + Low Confidence → Draft PR"
  "Intern: High Risk + Low Confidence → Suggestion Only"
  "Restricted: High Risk + High Confidence → LEVEL 3: Human Required"
