The Raptors and the Fence — Darkloom Cinematic Poster

Screen print / silkscreen poster art. Flat color blocks, NO gradients.
COLORS: Duotone — Deep Teal (#0A6E6E) and Terracotta (#E05C3A) on Off-Black (#121212). Limited to 3 colors.
TEXTURE: Heavy halftone dot patterns, paper grain, ink bleed at edges, slight misregistration.
COMPOSITION: Single symbolic focal point. Centered composition with 60% negative space. NOT literal illustration.
FIGURES: Silhouettes only, no detailed faces, stencil-cut edges.
TYPOGRAPHY: Bold condensed sans-serif integrated into composition (not overlaid).

FOCAL POINT: Centered geometric composition — a lattice grid structure (the fence) rendered in Deep Teal with halftone dot shading, framing a contained dark silhouette shape behind it. The silhouette suggests mass and capability but is geometrically contained within the lattice. The lattice grid extends slightly beyond the silhouette, implying the structure holds regardless of what's inside.

Negative space at top and bottom. The grid lines create a sense of precision, engineering, deliberate design.

TITLE TEXT (top, large bold condensed sans-serif, Deep Teal): "DARKLOOM"

SUBTITLE (below title, smaller, Terracotta): "THE FENCE THAT HOLDS"

BOTTOM TEXT (small, white halftone): "Own Your Mind, for the night is dark and full of terrors."

No literal raptors. No literal fences. Pure symbolic composition — a contained force behind a deliberately engineered lattice. Cold. Precise. Cinematic.

ASPECT: 9:16 (portrait poster)
